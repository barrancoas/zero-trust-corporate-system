## 1. Introduction

This manual describes how to access and use the corporate services provided by the Zero Trust Corporate System. All services are accessible exclusively through the corporate portal at `https://abb-ztcs.com`.

Authentication is centralised — you log in once and gain access to all services. Every login requires two factors:

1. **Your Active Directory password** — the same password used for your corporate account
2. **A one-time code** — generated by an authenticator app on your mobile device (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or similar)

---

## 2. First Access — Setting Up MFA

The first time you log in, the system will require you to enrol your mobile device as a second authentication factor. This only needs to be done once.

1. Open a browser and navigate to `https://abb-ztcs.com`
2. You are redirected to the corporate login page
3. Enter your username and password
4. The system displays a **QR code** — open your authenticator app and scan it
5. Enter the 6-digit code shown in the app to confirm enrolment
6. Give your device a name (e.g. `work-iphone`) and click **Submit**
7. You are redirected to the Nextcloud dashboard

> **Important:** keep your authenticator app installed and accessible. If you lose access to your device, contact your system administrator to reset your MFA.

---

## 3. Daily Login

1. Open a browser and navigate to `https://abb-ztcs.com`
2. You are redirected to the Keycloak login page — enter your username and password
3. Enter the 6-digit TOTP code from your authenticator app
4. You are redirected to the **Nextcloud dashboard**

![Corporate portal — Nextcloud dashboard after login](../../media/sso-config-01-dashboard.png)

> **Tip:** always use a supported browser (Firefox, Chrome, Edge). Private/incognito mode works but will require full login each time.

## 4. Nextcloud — Document Management

Nextcloud is the corporate document management platform. Use it to store, organise and share work files securely.

### 4.1 Accessing your files

After login, click **Files** in the left sidebar or the files icon in the top navigation bar. Your personal file storage is displayed.

### 4.2 Uploading a file

1. Navigate to the folder where you want to upload
2. Click the **+** button (top left, near the breadcrumb) → **Upload file**
3. Select the file from your computer
4. The file appears in your file list once uploaded

### 4.3 Creating a folder

1. Click **+** → **New folder**
2. Enter a name and press **Enter**

### 4.4 Downloading a file

1. Hover over the file name
2. Click the **three-dot menu** (⋮) → **Download**

### 4.5 Sharing a file with another user

1. Hover over the file → click the **Share** icon (person with +)
2. Type the username of the recipient (e.g. `bob.jones`)
3. Select the user from the dropdown
4. Choose permissions (can edit / view only)
5. Click **Share**

The recipient will see the shared file in their **Shared with me** section.

### 4.6 Viewing shared files

Click **Shared** in the left sidebar to see files shared with you or by you.

## 5. Mattermost — Corporate Messaging

Mattermost is the corporate messaging platform. Use it for team communication, direct messages and file sharing.

### 5.1 Accessing Mattermost

Navigate to `https://abb-ztcs.com/mattermost/` in your browser.

> **Note:** Mattermost uses a separate local account. Contact your administrator for your Mattermost credentials if you have not received them.

### 5.2 Basic usage

- **Channels** — click a channel name in the left sidebar to open it and read or post messages
- **Direct messages** — click **+** next to **Direct Messages** → search for a colleague → start a conversation
- **Send a message** — type in the message box at the bottom and press **Enter**
- **Share a file** — click the **paperclip** icon in the message box to attach a file

---

## 6. Logging Out

### 6.1 Logging out of Nextcloud

1. Click your **avatar** (top right corner)
2. Click **Log out**
3. The browser redirects to the Keycloak logout page — your session is terminated across all services

### 6.2 Session expiry

If you leave your session inactive for an extended period, the system will automatically log you out. Navigate to `https://abb-ztcs.com` to log in again.

## 7. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| "Unexpected error when handling authentication request" | DC01 may be offline — contact your administrator |
| Lost access to authenticator app | Contact your administrator to reset MFA |
| Cannot access `https://abb-ztcs.com` | Check your internet connection — the service requires HTTPS on port 443 |
| File upload fails | Check file size — large files may time out. Try again or split into smaller files |
| Mattermost shows blank page | Clear browser cache and reload |

---

## 8. Security Guidelines

- **Never share your password** with anyone, including IT staff
- **Never share your TOTP codes** — they are single-use and expire in 30 seconds
- **Always log out** when using a shared or public computer
- **Report suspicious activity** to your administrator immediately — unexpected login prompts, unknown devices in your authenticator app, or files you did not create
